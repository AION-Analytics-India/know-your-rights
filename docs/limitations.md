# Limitations

These are real and current. They are listed because a legal service that hides
its gaps is the failure mode it exists to avoid.

## Coverage

- **Coverage is incomplete.** Labour, family, disability and senior-citizen
  rights are thin or absent. The service declines rather than improvises, but a
  decline is still an unanswered question.
- **State coverage is uneven.** State-specific material is deepest for Gujarat.
  Users elsewhere may receive a national provision where a state law actually
  governs. Where a rule is state-limited, the answer says so — read that.
- **Some law is not in any statute.** Whether a particular thing meets a
  statutory test is often settled by judgments rather than legislation, and this
  service indexes statutes. It can name the test and say who decides it; it
  cannot tell you how a court has decided.
- **Answers reflect the law as recorded in the sources loaded**, which may lag
  recent amendments.

## Language

- **Three languages only**: English, Hindi and Gujarati.
- **Hindi and Gujarati answers are weaker than English ones.** They cite
  correctly but are less consistent about stating the next practical step, and
  translation errors have been found in answers that cited the right provision.
  **Treat the citation as the reliable part and check the wording against it.**
- **Recognition of a described situation is partly phrase-based.** An unusual
  wording may get the general answer without the specific remedy attached, and
  the non-English phrasings have not all been reviewed by a native speaker.

## What it is not

- **Not a lawyer.** It gives information and next steps. It cannot assess your
  specific situation, represent you, or tell you what the outcome will be.
- **Not an emergency service.** If you are in immediate danger, call the police
  (100 / 112) or the relevant helpline.
- **Not validated for open public use.** Release is gated on review by practising
  advocates, domain by domain. Until that is done, treat every answer as a
  starting point for checking, not as a conclusion.

## Reporting a wrong answer

A wrong legal answer is the most serious defect this project can have. Please
open an issue with the question, the language, the answer given and what it
should have said. Please do not include personal details of a real situation.
